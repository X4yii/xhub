# ModularVoiceX4yi v1.0.0

[ES]
### Motor de Audio Nativo en PipeWire (`mvxd` & `mvx-audio`)
- **Micrófono Virtual Nativo (`mvx_mic`):** Creación directa de fuente virtual `Audio/Source/Virtual` sobre `libpipewire-0.3` sin depender de `pw-loopback` ni `pactl`.
- **Enlaces Explícitos (`link-factory`):** Ruteo determinista de canales `FL/FR` entre `mvx.output` y `mvx_mic` inmune a políticas erróneas del gestor de sesión.
- **Procesamiento Tiempo Real Lock-Free:** Callbacks RT (`PW_STREAM_FLAG_RT_PROCESS`) con cero asignaciones de memoria, anillos SPSC `rtrb` y telemetría atómica.

### Rack DSP y 14 Presets Integrados
- **Pitch y Formantes Independientes:** Integración de *Signalsmith Stretch* con tres perfiles de calidad (`Ultra`, `Equilibrado`, `Alta`) y bypass automático de latencia cuando no hay desplazamiento tonal.
- **Cadena de 14 Módulos DSP:** `NoiseReduction` (DeepFilterNet3), `SpeakerLock`, `NeuralVoice`, `HighPass`, `NoiseGate`, `Equalizer` (3 bandas), `Compressor`, `Humanizer`, `Chorus`, `Robotizer`, `RingMod`, `Distortion` (con bitcrusher), `Reverb` (Freeverb) y `Gain`.
- **14 Presets de Fábrica:** `natural`, `broadcast`, `voz_grave`, `voz_ardilla`, `titan`, `cyborg`, `police_radio`, `megaphone`, `cave_echo`, `anonymous`, `alien`, `telephone`, `helium` y `radio_demon`.
- **Soundboard y Loop Tester:** Sintetizador integrado de 4 efectos (`airhorn`, `bleep`, `chime`, `buzzer`), cargador de `.wav` personalizados, Sidetone en vivo y grabador de bucle de 8 segundos.

### Inteligencia Artificial Local (ONNX / CPU)
- **DeepFilterNet3:** Reemplazo del filtro de ruido legado por supresión neuronal `DeepFilterNet3` en tiempo real sobre `tract`.
- **Biometría Vocal ("Solo mi voz"):** Verificación del hablante fuera del hilo RT mediante **Silero VAD** y **CAM++** (`sherpa-onnx`) con enrolamiento de 10s, umbral coseno, `hold_ms` y `lookahead_ms`.
- **Clonación Neuronal de Timbre `[ALPHA]`:** Integración de **OpenVoice V2** (`tone_extract.onnx` y `tone_color.onnx`) con estimación automática de $F_0$ por autocorrelación, formantes y balance espectral de 3 bandas.

### Clientes (`mvx` TUI/CLI, `mvx-tray`) e Instalador
- **TUI Interactiva (`ratatui`):** Soporte completo de ratón y teclado, analizador de espectro de 8 bandas, vúmetros RMS/Peak y medidores de reducción de ganancia.
- **CLI y Waybar:** Subcomandos rápidos `mvx ctl`, diagnóstico `mvx doctor`, clonación `mvx clone` y salida JSON nativa `mvx status --waybar`.
- **Bandeja del Sistema e Instalador:** Cliente SNI `mvx-tray`, unidades `systemd --user` y scripts automatizados `install.sh` / `uninstall.sh`.
[/ES]

[EN]
### Native PipeWire Audio Engine (`mvxd` & `mvx-audio`)
- **Native Virtual Microphone (`mvx_mic`):** Direct creation of an `Audio/Source/Virtual` node via `libpipewire-0.3` without relying on `pw-loopback` or `pactl`.
- **Explicit Port Linking (`link-factory`):** Deterministic `FL/FR` port wiring between `mvx.output` and `mvx_mic`, bypassing session manager auto-routing pitfalls.
- **Lock-Free Real-Time Processing:** RT callbacks (`PW_STREAM_FLAG_RT_PROCESS`) with zero heap allocations, `rtrb` SPSC ring buffers, and lock-free atomic telemetry.

### DSP Rack & 14 Built-in Presets
- **Independent Pitch & Formant Shifting:** Powered by *Signalsmith Stretch* with three quality profiles (`Ultra`, `Balanced`, `High`) and automatic zero-latency bypass when unshifted.
- **14-Module DSP Filter Chain:** `NoiseReduction` (DeepFilterNet3), `SpeakerLock`, `NeuralVoice`, `HighPass`, `NoiseGate`, 3-band `Equalizer`, `Compressor`, `Humanizer`, `Chorus`, `Robotizer`, `RingMod`, `Distortion` (with bitcrusher), `Reverb` (Freeverb), and `Gain`.
- **14 Factory Presets:** `natural`, `broadcast`, `voz_grave`, `voz_ardilla`, `titan`, `cyborg`, `police_radio`, `megaphone`, `cave_echo`, `anonymous`, `alien`, `telephone`, `helium`, and `radio_demon`.
- **Soundboard & Loop Tester:** Built-in synthesizer for 4 sound effects (`airhorn`, `bleep`, `chime`, `buzzer`), custom `.wav` loader, live Sidetone monitor, and 8-second voice loop recorder.

### Local AI Engine (ONNX / CPU)
- **DeepFilterNet3:** Replaced legacy spectral noise filter with real-time `DeepFilterNet3` neural noise suppression powered by `tract`.
- **Vocal Biometrics ("My Voice Only"):** Out-of-thread speaker verification using **Silero VAD** and **CAM++** (`sherpa-onnx`) with 10s voice enrollment, cosine threshold, `hold_ms`, and `lookahead_ms`.
- **Neural Timbre Cloning `[ALPHA]`:** Integrated **OpenVoice V2** (`tone_extract.onnx` and `tone_color.onnx`) with automatic autocorrelation $F_0$ pitch detection, formant estimation, and 3-band spectral EQ matching.

### Clients (`mvx` TUI/CLI, `mvx-tray`) & Installer
- **Interactive TUI (`ratatui`):** Full mouse and keyboard control, 8-band spectrum analyzer, RMS/Peak VU meters, and Gate/Compressor gain reduction meters.
- **CLI & Waybar Support:** Instant `mvx ctl` hotkeys, `mvx doctor` diagnostics, `mvx clone` CLI workflow, and native `mvx status --waybar` JSON output.
- **System Tray & Installer:** StatusNotifierItem `mvx-tray` daemon, `systemd --user` services, and automated `install.sh` / `uninstall.sh` scripts.
[/EN]
