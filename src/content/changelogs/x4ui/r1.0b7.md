# X4UI r1.0b7

[ES]
### Nuevas Características de Layout y Renderizado
- **Radios por Esquina y Bordes Selectivos (`CornerRadii` y `BorderSides`):** `IGraphics` y `GLGraphics` ahora soportan `drawRoundedRect(x, y, w, h, CornerRadii, color)`, `drawRoundedBorder(...)` y `drawBorder(..., BorderSides)`. `GuiPanel` expone `setCornerRadii(...)`, `setBorderSides(...)` y `setSuppressChrome(boolean)` para uniones visuales limpias.
- **Paneles Unidos y Alas Acopladas (`GuiUnionPanel`, `DockSide`, `GuiBaseContainer.attachSidePanel`):**
  - `GuiUnionPanel` compone un cuerpo central con alas acopladas (`DockSide.LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `TOP`, `BOTTOM`) sin costuras internas ni sobrepintado de fondo, calculando `getUnionBounds()` y un `isClippedBy(IGraphics)` exacto para no descartar alas visibles.
  - `GuiBaseContainer.attachSidePanel(...)` ancla paneles laterales alrededor de la ventana principal y sincroniza automáticamente `hasClickedOutside(...)` y las áreas de exclusión de JEI.
- **Motor Flex Avanzado y Grillas (`FlexLayout`, `GuiGridPanel`):**
  - `FlexLayout` incorpora distribución water-filling con `flexGrow`, `flexShrink`, cotas `minWidth`/`maxWidth`/`minHeight`/`maxHeight` y sincronización de layout.
  - Nuevo `GuiGridPanel` con columnas fijas o `autoColumns`, espaciado configurable y recálculo perezoso basado en dirty flag.

### Ergonomía de API para Mods Consumidores
- **Rediseño de `GuiBuilder` como Punto de Entrada Oficial:** Oculta `GuiScreen` y `Minecraft.getMinecraft().displayGuiScreen(...)` a los mods consumidores mediante `GuiBuilder.screen(title)` (`window`, `dimBackground`, `dimColor`, `animateOpen`, `escCloses`, `pauseGame`, `bindChannel`, `theme`, `onInit`, `onResize`, `onClose`, `build`, `open`), `GuiBuilder.register(id, factory)`, `GuiBuilder.modal(title, w, h, body)`, `GuiBuilder.open(screen)` y `GuiBuilder.close()`.
- **Composición sin Casts (`GuiComponent.add` + Factories `.of(...)`):**
  - `GuiComponent.add(T child)` y `add(T child, Consumer<T> config)` (delegados también en `GuiScrollPanel`).
  - Factories flex-first en los widgets principales: `GuiButton.of(...)`, `GuiLabel.of(...)`, `GuiTextInput.of(...)`, `GuiToggle.of(...)`, `GuiSliderInt.of(...)`, `GuiSliderFloat.of(...)`, `GuiSliderDouble.of(...)`, `GuiDropdown.of(...)`, `GuiUnionPanel.of(...)`, `GuiVirtualList.of(...)`, `GuiMarkdown.of(...)`, `GuiImage.of(...)`/`ofUrl(...)`, `GuiVideo.of(...)`, `GuiSlot.of(...)` y `GuiModelRender.of(...)`.
  - Retornos covariantes en setters fluidos (`withPercentWidth`, `withMargin`, `withFlexGrow`, `withFlexShrink`, `withMinSize`, `withMaxSize`, `withTooltip`) eliminando casts repetitivos.

### Estabilidad, Concurrencia y Rendimiento
- **`ScreenHost` Unificado:** Extrae la lógica compartida entre `GuiBaseScreen` y `GuiBaseContainer` (gestión de RPCs y `UINetwork.sweepExpiredRpcs()` en ambas pantallas, `deltaTime`, tooltips cacheados sin `split("\n")` por frame, animación de apertura `setAnimateOpen(boolean)` independiente de FPS y limpieza en `onGuiClosed()`).
- **Concurrencia y Widgets:**
  - `State<T>` ahora es thread-safe (`volatile` + serialización de notificaciones en `set(T)`).
  - `GuiMarkdown` recalcula el ajuste de líneas automáticamente en `onBoundsChanged()` al cambiar de ancho bajo layouts flex/porcentuales.
  - `GuiVirtualList` invalida su rango al cambiar tamaño y recicla vistas activas en `setItems(...)`.
  - `UINetwork.clearPlayerRateLimits(UUID)` libera memoria al desconectarse un jugador desde `ClientSessionTracker`.
  - `GuiAnimator` reemplaza `CopyOnWriteArrayList` por una lista sincronizada sin copias completas por frame y registra excepciones con Log4j2.
- **Optimizaciones por Frame y Heap:**
  - `GuiBaseContainer` (`sidePanelsDirty`), `GuiGridPanel` (layout bajo demanda) y `GuiOverlayManager` (caché de overlays activos) eliminan asignaciones repetitivas por fotograma.
  - `GLGraphics.drawBorder(..., BorderSides)` agrupa todos los lados activos en un único draw call OpenGL.
  - `TrueTypeFont` libera `atlasImage` (`~4 MB` en heap por fuente) inmediatamente tras subir la textura a GPU.
  - `GuiComponent` cachea `absX`/`absY` con invalidación en cascada.

### Seguridad, Multimedia y Runtime
- **Endurecimiento de Seguridad y Red (`SecurityUtils`, `ActionGuard`, `Packet*`):**
  - `SecurityUtils` refuerza la validación contra SSRF (bloqueo de rangos IPv4/IPv6 privados, loopback, link-local y multicast), redirecciones inseguras y path-traversal.
  - `ActionGuard`, `PacketGuiAction`, `PacketGuiRpc`, `PacketHandshake` y `StateContainerListener` añaden validaciones de contexto/rango/límites más estrictas.
- **Estado Reactivo y Multimedia:**
  - `State<T>` aísla excepciones entre listeners durante `set(T)` y `UIStateBus` refuerza la seguridad de tipos y alcances (`SCREEN`/`SESSION`/`GLOBAL`).
  - `MediaPipeline`, `OpenALAudioSink`, `DirectMemoryVideoDecoder`, `FFmpegAudioDecoder` y `JCodecVideoDecoder` corrigen fugas de buffers/procesos nativos, sincronización de audio/video y limpieza idempotente.
  - `PlatformBridge` (Java 8 y Java 21) corrige el manejo de recursos nativos y compatibilidad con CleanroomMC.

### Breaking Changes y Limpieza Beta
- **Eliminación de código muerto/legacy:** se eliminaron `IMediaService`, `MediaService`, `IMediaAsset`, `AssetCallback`, `RemoteResourceManager`, `DownloadManager`, `FFmpegProcessDecoder`, `IGuiActionSender`, `GuiCard`, `GuiTooltip`, `GuiBaseScreen.drawTitle()`/`drawBackground()`, los métodos estáticos legacy de `GuiComponent` y el antiguo `GuiBuilder<T>`.
- `cleanCache(modId, daysOld)` se migró a `MediaPipeline.getInstance().cleanCache(modId, daysOld)`.
- `GuiPanel.spacer()` ahora opera directamente sobre `flexGrow = 1.0f`.
- **Distribución Maven recomendada:** se adopta **Modrinth Maven** (`https://api.modrinth.com/maven` con `rfg.deobf("maven.modrinth:x4ui:r1.0b7")`) como canal oficial para mods consumidores.

### New Layout & Rendering Features
- **Per-Corner Radii & Selective Borders (`CornerRadii` & `BorderSides`):** `IGraphics` and `GLGraphics` now support `drawRoundedRect(x, y, w, h, CornerRadii, color)`, `drawRoundedBorder(...)`, and `drawBorder(..., BorderSides)`. `GuiPanel` exposes `setCornerRadii(...)`, `setBorderSides(...)`, and `setSuppressChrome(boolean)` for seamless panel joints.
- **Unified Panels & Docked Wings (`GuiUnionPanel`, `DockSide`, `GuiBaseContainer.attachSidePanel`):**
  - `GuiUnionPanel` composes a central body with docked wings (`DockSide.LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `TOP`, `BOTTOM`) with disjoint background fills, outer-only borders, `getUnionBounds()`, and accurate `isClippedBy(IGraphics)` culling so visible wings are never clipped prematurely.
  - `GuiBaseContainer.attachSidePanel(...)` anchors side panels around the main window and automatically syncs `hasClickedOutside(...)` and JEI exclusion zones.
- **Advanced Flex Engine & Grids (`FlexLayout`, `GuiGridPanel`):**
  - `FlexLayout` adds water-filling distribution via `flexGrow` and `flexShrink`, plus `minWidth`/`maxWidth`/`minHeight`/`maxHeight` bounds.
  - New `GuiGridPanel` with fixed columns or `autoColumns`, configurable spacing, and dirty-flag layout recalculation.

### Consumer Mod API Ergonomics
- **Redesigned `GuiBuilder` Official Entry Point:** Hides `GuiScreen` and `Minecraft.getMinecraft().displayGuiScreen(...)` from consumer mods via `GuiBuilder.screen(title)` (`window`, `dimBackground`, `dimColor`, `animateOpen`, `escCloses`, `pauseGame`, `bindChannel`, `theme`, `onInit`, `onResize`, `onClose`, `build`, `open`), `GuiBuilder.register(id, factory)`, `GuiBuilder.modal(title, w, h, body)`, `GuiBuilder.open(screen)`, and `GuiBuilder.close()`.
- **Cast-Free Composition (`GuiComponent.add` + `.of(...)` Factories):**
  - `GuiComponent.add(T child)` and `add(T child, Consumer<T> config)` (also delegated in `GuiScrollPanel`).
  - Flex-first factories on core widgets: `GuiButton.of(...)`, `GuiLabel.of(...)`, `GuiTextInput.of(...)`, `GuiToggle.of(...)`, `GuiSliderInt.of(...)`, `GuiSliderFloat.of(...)`, `GuiSliderDouble.of(...)`, `GuiDropdown.of(...)`, `GuiUnionPanel.of(...)`, `GuiVirtualList.of(...)`, `GuiMarkdown.of(...)`, `GuiImage.of(...)`/`ofUrl(...)`, `GuiVideo.of(...)`, `GuiSlot.of(...)`, and `GuiModelRender.of(...)`.
  - Covariant return types on common fluent setters (`withPercentWidth`, `withMargin`, `withFlexGrow`, `withFlexShrink`, `withMinSize`, `withMaxSize`, `withTooltip`), eliminating manual casts.

### Stability, Concurrency & Performance
- **Unified `ScreenHost`:** Extracts shared screen logic between `GuiBaseScreen` and `GuiBaseContainer` (unified RPC tracking and `UINetwork.sweepExpiredRpcs()` in both screens, `deltaTime`, cached tooltips without per-frame `split("\n")`, FPS-independent `setAnimateOpen(boolean)` zoom-in, and `onGuiClosed()` cleanup).
- **Concurrency & Widget Fixes:**
  - `State<T>` is now thread-safe (`volatile` value + serialized delivery in `set(T)`).
  - `GuiMarkdown` automatically re-wraps lines in `onBoundsChanged()` when width changes under flex or percentage layouts.
  - `GuiVirtualList` invalidates its visible window on resize and recycles active views inside `setItems(...)`.
  - `UINetwork.clearPlayerRateLimits(UUID)` evicts per-player rate-limit entries on disconnect via `ClientSessionTracker`.
  - `GuiAnimator` replaces `CopyOnWriteArrayList` with a synchronized list avoiding per-frame full copies and logs exceptions through Log4j2.
- **Per-Frame & Heap Optimizations:**
  - `GuiBaseContainer` (`sidePanelsDirty`), `GuiGridPanel` (lazy dirty-flag layout), and `GuiOverlayManager` (active overlay caching) eliminate repetitive per-frame allocations.
  - `GLGraphics.drawBorder(..., BorderSides)` batches all active sides into a single OpenGL draw call.
  - `TrueTypeFont` releases `atlasImage` (`~4 MB` heap per font) immediately after GPU upload.
  - `GuiComponent` caches absolute `absX`/`absY` coordinates with cascading invalidation.

### Security, Media & Runtime
- **Security & Network Hardening (`SecurityUtils`, `ActionGuard`, `Packet*`):**
  - `SecurityUtils` hardens SSRF checks (blocking private, loopback, link-local, and multicast IPv4/IPv6 ranges), unsafe redirects, and path-traversal attempts.
  - `ActionGuard`, `PacketGuiAction`, `PacketGuiRpc`, `PacketHandshake`, and `StateContainerListener` enforce stricter payload, bounds, and session checks.
- **Reactive State & Media Pipeline:**
  - `State<T>` isolates per-listener exceptions during `set(T)` delivery, and `UIStateBus` enforces scope and type safety.
  - `MediaPipeline`, `OpenALAudioSink`, `DirectMemoryVideoDecoder`, `FFmpegAudioDecoder`, and `JCodecVideoDecoder` fix native buffer/process leaks, A/V sync edge cases, and idempotent cleanup.
  - `PlatformBridge` (Java 8 & Java 21) improves native cleanup and CleanroomMC compatibility.

### Breaking Changes & Beta Cleanup
- **Dead/Legacy Code Removal:** removed `IMediaService`, `MediaService`, `IMediaAsset`, `AssetCallback`, `RemoteResourceManager`, `DownloadManager`, `FFmpegProcessDecoder`, `IGuiActionSender`, `GuiCard`, `GuiTooltip`, `GuiBaseScreen.drawTitle()`/`drawBackground()`, legacy static helpers in `GuiComponent`, and the old `GuiBuilder<T>`.
- Migrated `cleanCache(modId, daysOld)` to `MediaPipeline.getInstance().cleanCache(modId, daysOld)`.
- Simplified `GuiPanel.spacer()` to operate directly via `flexGrow = 1.0f`.
- **Recommended Maven Distribution:** **Modrinth Maven** (`https://api.modrinth.com/maven` with `rfg.deobf("maven.modrinth:x4ui:r1.0b7")`) is now the official recommended repository for consumer mods.
