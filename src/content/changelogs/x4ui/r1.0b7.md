# X4UI r1.0b7

[ES]
### Arquitectura de Pantallas y Paneles Laterales (`ScreenHost` & `GuiBaseContainer`)
- **Nuevo `ScreenHost`:** Extrae y unifica el controlador común entre `GuiBaseScreen` y `GuiBaseContainer` (árbol de componentes, overlays, RPCs, toasts, entrada y ciclo de vida).
- **`GuiBaseContainer` con paridad completa:**
  - Soporte nativo de `attachSidePanel(DockSide, ...)` (`requestSidePanelLayout()`, `layoutSidePanelsAndSync()`), `createCenteredWindow(maxWidth, maxHeight)`, `hasClickedOutside(...)` (integración limpia con paneles laterales y JEI), `removeComponent(...)` y `setAnimateOpen(boolean)`.
- **Nuevo `GuiBuilder` fluido:** Reescritura del constructor declarativo (reemplaza al antiguo `GuiBuilder<T>`) con helpers modernos para ventanas, paneles, botones y bindings reactivos.

### Motor de Layout, Geometría y Nuevos Contenedores
- **Restricciones y Flexbox en `GuiComponent` + `FlexLayout`:**
  - `GuiComponent` añade `minWidth`, `maxWidth`, `minHeight`, `maxHeight`, `baseWidth`/`baseHeight`, `flexGrow`, `flexShrink` y setters fluidos (`withMinWidth`, `withMaxWidth`, `withMinSize`, `withMaxSize`, `withFlexGrow`, `withFlexShrink`, `withFlex`), además de `add(child, Consumer<T>)`, `invalidateAbs()`, `isClippedBy(IGraphics)` y `onBoundsChanged()`.
  - `FlexLayout` implementa distribución *water-filling* para `flexGrow`/`flexShrink` respetando límites `min`/`max` y corrigiendo cálculo de bounds y padding.
- **Primitivas `CornerRadii`, `BorderSides` y `DockSide`:**
  - `IGraphics` y `GLGraphics` incorporan renderizado de esquinas independientes (`CornerRadii`) y bordes selectivos (`BorderSides`).
  - `GuiPanel` añade soporte de `CornerRadii`, `BorderSides`, `suppressChrome` y tarjetas temáticas.
- **`GuiGridPanel`:** Nuevo contenedor en grilla con `autoColumns` para distribuir tarjetas o slots de forma adaptable.
- **`GuiUnionPanel`:** Nuevo contenedor que fusiona visualmente paneles acoplados (`DockSide`) con esquinas y bordes continuos.

### Componentes, Renderizado y Temas
- **`GuiDropdown`:** Soporte de navegación por teclado (`onKeyPress` con flechas, Enter y Escape), `highlightedIndex`, constructor estático `GuiDropdown.of(State<T>, List<T>)` y mejoras de hit-testing/overlay.
- **Correcciones en Widgets y Render:** Mejoras de clipping, foco, estado y renderizado en `GuiAnimator`, `GuiToastManager`, `GuiOverlayManager`, `GuiVideo`, `GuiAudio`, `GuiVirtualList`, `GuiModelRender`, `GuiMarkdown`, `GuiTextInput`, `GuiButton`, `GuiImage`, `GuiLabel`, `GuiScrollPanel`, `GuiSlot`, `GuiToggle`, `DefaultTheme` y sliders (`GuiSlider*`).

### Seguridad, Red, Multimedia y Runtime
- **Endurecimiento de Seguridad y Red (`SecurityUtils`, `ActionGuard`, `Packet*`, `UIChannels`):**
  - `SecurityUtils` refuerza la protección contra SSRF (bloqueo de IPv4/IPv6 privadas, loopback, link-local y multicast), redirecciones inseguras y path-traversal.
  - `ActionGuard`, `PacketGuiAction`, `PacketGuiRpc`, `PacketHandshake` y `StateContainerListener` añaden validaciones estrictas de payload, rangos y sesión.
  - `UIChannels`, `UINetwork`, `ClientSessionTracker`, `ClientProxy` y `CommonProxy` optimizan la gestión de canales y limpieza de sesiones.
- **Estado Reactivo y Multimedia:**
  - `State<T>` aísla excepciones entre listeners durante `set(T)` y `UIStateBus` refuerza la seguridad de tipos y alcances (`SCREEN`/`SESSION`/`GLOBAL`).
  - `MediaPipeline`, `OpenALAudioSink`, `DirectMemoryVideoDecoder`, `FFmpegAudioDecoder` y `JCodecVideoDecoder` corrigen fugas de buffers/procesos nativos, sincronización A/V y limpieza idempotente.
  - `PlatformBridge` (Java 8 y Java 21) mejora la limpieza de recursos nativos y compatibilidad con CleanroomMC.

### Breaking Changes y Limpieza Beta
- **Eliminación de código muerto/legacy:** se eliminaron `IMediaService`, `MediaService`, `IMediaAsset`, `AssetCallback`, `RemoteResourceManager`, `DownloadManager`, `FFmpegProcessDecoder`, `IGuiActionSender`, `GuiCard`, `GuiTooltip`, `GuiBaseScreen.drawTitle()`/`drawBackground()`, los métodos estáticos legacy de `GuiComponent` y el antiguo `GuiBuilder<T>`.
- `cleanCache(modId, daysOld)` se migró a `MediaPipeline.getInstance().cleanCache(modId, daysOld)`.
- `GuiPanel.spacer()` ahora opera directamente sobre `flexGrow = 1.0f`.
- **Distribución Maven recomendada:** se adopta **Modrinth Maven** (`https://api.modrinth.com/maven` con `rfg.deobf("maven.modrinth:x4ui:r1.0b7")`) como canal oficial para mods consumidores.

[EN]
### Screen Architecture & Side Panels (`ScreenHost` & `GuiBaseContainer`)
- **New `ScreenHost`:** Extracts and unifies the shared controller behind `GuiBaseScreen` and `GuiBaseContainer` (component tree, overlays, RPCs, toasts, input routing, and lifecycle).
- **Full `GuiBaseContainer` parity:**
  - Native support for `attachSidePanel(DockSide, ...)` (`requestSidePanelLayout()`, `layoutSidePanelsAndSync()`), `createCenteredWindow(maxWidth, maxHeight)`, `hasClickedOutside(...)` (clean JEI and side-panel hit-testing), `removeComponent(...)`, and `setAnimateOpen(boolean)`.
- **New fluent `GuiBuilder`:** Rewritten non-generic declarative builder (replacing the old `GuiBuilder<T>`) with modern helpers for windows, panels, buttons, and reactive bindings.

### Layout Engine, Geometry & New Containers
- **Constraints & Flexbox in `GuiComponent` + `FlexLayout`:**
  - `GuiComponent` adds `minWidth`, `maxWidth`, `minHeight`, `maxHeight`, `baseWidth`/`baseHeight`, `flexGrow`, `flexShrink`, and fluent setters (`withMinWidth`, `withMaxWidth`, `withMinSize`, `withMaxSize`, `withFlexGrow`, `withFlexShrink`, `withFlex`), plus `add(child, Consumer<T>)`, `invalidateAbs()`, `isClippedBy(IGraphics)`, and `onBoundsChanged()`.
  - `FlexLayout` implements water-filling distribution for `flexGrow`/`flexShrink` respecting `min`/`max` constraints and fixing bounds and padding calculations.
- **`CornerRadii`, `BorderSides` & `DockSide` primitives:**
  - `IGraphics` and `GLGraphics` add per-corner rounding (`CornerRadii`) and selective border rendering (`BorderSides`).
  - `GuiPanel` supports `CornerRadii`, `BorderSides`, `suppressChrome`, and themed cards.
- **`GuiGridPanel`:** New grid container with `autoColumns` for responsive card or slot grids.
- **`GuiUnionPanel`:** New container that visually merges docked panels (`DockSide`) with continuous corners and borders.

### Components, Rendering & Themes
- **`GuiDropdown`:** Full keyboard navigation (`onKeyPress` with arrow keys, Enter, and Escape), `highlightedIndex`, static `GuiDropdown.of(State<T>, List<T>)`, and hit-testing/overlay fixes.
- **Widget & Render Fixes:** Clipping, focus, state, and rendering fixes across `GuiAnimator`, `GuiToastManager`, `GuiOverlayManager`, `GuiVideo`, `GuiAudio`, `GuiVirtualList`, `GuiModelRender`, `GuiMarkdown`, `GuiTextInput`, `GuiButton`, `GuiImage`, `GuiLabel`, `GuiScrollPanel`, `GuiSlot`, `GuiToggle`, `DefaultTheme`, and typed sliders (`GuiSlider*`).

### Security, Networking, Media & Runtime
- **Security & Network Hardening (`SecurityUtils`, `ActionGuard`, `Packet*`, `UIChannels`):**
  - `SecurityUtils` hardens SSRF checks (blocking private, loopback, link-local, and multicast IPv4/IPv6 ranges), unsafe redirects, and path-traversal attempts.
  - `ActionGuard`, `PacketGuiAction`, `PacketGuiRpc`, `PacketHandshake`, and `StateContainerListener` enforce stricter payload, bounds, and session checks.
  - `UIChannels`, `UINetwork`, `ClientSessionTracker`, `ClientProxy`, and `CommonProxy` streamline channel management and session cleanup.
- **Reactive State & Media Pipeline:**
  - `State<T>` isolates per-listener exceptions during `set(T)` delivery, and `UIStateBus` enforces scope (`SCREEN`/`SESSION`/`GLOBAL`) and type safety.
  - `MediaPipeline`, `OpenALAudioSink`, `DirectMemoryVideoDecoder`, `FFmpegAudioDecoder`, and `JCodecVideoDecoder` fix native buffer/process leaks, A/V sync edge cases, and idempotent cleanup.
  - `PlatformBridge` (Java 8 & Java 21) improves native resource cleanup and CleanroomMC compatibility.

### Breaking Changes & Beta Cleanup
- **Dead/Legacy Code Removal:** removed `IMediaService`, `MediaService`, `IMediaAsset`, `AssetCallback`, `RemoteResourceManager`, `DownloadManager`, `FFmpegProcessDecoder`, `IGuiActionSender`, `GuiCard`, `GuiTooltip`, `GuiBaseScreen.drawTitle()`/`drawBackground()`, legacy static helpers in `GuiComponent`, and the old `GuiBuilder<T>`.
- Migrated `cleanCache(modId, daysOld)` to `MediaPipeline.getInstance().cleanCache(modId, daysOld)`.
- Simplified `GuiPanel.spacer()` to operate directly via `flexGrow = 1.0f`.
- **Recommended Maven Distribution:** **Modrinth Maven** (`https://api.modrinth.com/maven` with `rfg.deobf("maven.modrinth:x4ui:r1.0b7")`) is now the official recommended repository for consumer mods.
